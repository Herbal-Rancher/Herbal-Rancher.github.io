Welcome to my Security+ portfolio! This is where I document my cybersecurity education at Calbright College, practical skills, projects, and preparation for CompTIA Security+.

## Purpose

My goal is to build upon my networking foundation and develop practical skills in securing systems, identifying threats, managing risk, and protecting enterprise infrastructure.

## Learning structure

The following areas are planned for development as my studies progress:

- Security+ certification study center and objective reviews
- Formative modules and technical assessments
- Hands-on cybersecurity labs and video walkthroughs
- Interactive study diagrams and technical references
- Security test readiness lab and original practice questions
- Cybersecurity projects and security operations

The certification exam version and detailed curriculum will be confirmed before building the Security+ study materials.

## Networking foundation

My [Network+ portfolio](/network-portfolio/) remains a separate learning journey. Its [Network+ Certification Study Center](/network-portfolio/network-plus/) includes foundational network security topics that support my cybersecurity studies.

**Learn. Practice. Test. Retest. Master.**
