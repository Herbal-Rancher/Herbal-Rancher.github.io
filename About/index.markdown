Welcome to my portfolio—a living record of learning, building, questioning, and discovery. I'm a Network Technology student and lifelong learner with interests spanning networking, cybersecurity, technical documentation, trading, Bible study, and systems thinking.

What began as a place to document what I was learning has grown into an evidence trail of **what I am learning, how I am learning it, and how my understanding develops over time**.

My goal is not to present myself as having every answer. It is to learn carefully, document honestly, test what I understand, and share what I discover along the way.

<div style="height: 3px; background: #f4b400; margin: 30px 0;"></div>

## Learning in Public

Throughout this portfolio, I document learning through:

* Hands-on labs and troubleshooting
* Video walkthroughs
* Study diagrams and technical notes
* Research and independent study
* Bible study and Scripture exploration
* Reflective and AI-assisted learning

I intentionally preserve more than finished results. Questions, mistakes, corrections, discoveries, and changes in understanding are part of the record too. A completed project shows **what I learned**. The journey behind it shows **how I learned it**.

---

## Partnering With AI

Artificial intelligence has become an important tool in my learning process. 

My work with ChatGPT is best described as a **study and creative partnership—not an authority relationship**. I use AI to explore questions, organize ideas, compare concepts, challenge assumptions, improve documentation, develop study resources, and turn complicated subjects into something I can examine more clearly.

But AI-generated information is not automatically accepted as truth. Depending on the subject, I test what I learn against technical documentation, course materials, hands-on results, independent research, primary sources, or—in Bible study—the biblical text itself. **AI supports my thinking; it does not replace it.**

The responsibility for determining what is accurate, useful, and worth keeping remains mine.

## Through Chat's Lens

The three images featured here began with a simple question I asked ChatGPT: **“Based on our conversations, create an image that represents me.”**

What makes the experiment especially interesting is that the first image was created **before I provided a personal photograph**. ChatGPT had only our conversations—the subjects I studied, questions I asked, projects I built, challenges I worked through, and recurring themes in how I approached learning.

Later images were created after sharing personal visual references and continuing those conversations.

I don't consider them literal portraits. They are **AI-generated visual reflections of a person interpreted through conversation**—faith, technology, curiosity, resilience, creativity, study, and continual growth seen through Chat's lens.

## A Final Reflection

This portfolio began as a place to document technical studies. It has become a record of **curiosity, creativity, perseverance, faith, and lifelong learning**. Every lab completed, diagram created, video recorded, passage studied, question explored, and mistake corrected represents another step forward.

The subjects throughout these pages may be different, but the purpose behind them is the same: **to understand things more deeply, document the journey honestly, and continue growing through what I learn.**

Ultimately, this portfolio represents both the person I aspire to become and the person God continues to shape through every season of life: **faithful, disciplined, compassionate, curious, resilient, technically capable, and committed to continual growth.**

I don't expect these pages to show a journey without mistakes. I hope they show something better:

**a willingness to keep learning.**

The projects are still evolving ... the portfolio is still growing.

**And so am I ... as are you. Let's learn and grow together!  **
