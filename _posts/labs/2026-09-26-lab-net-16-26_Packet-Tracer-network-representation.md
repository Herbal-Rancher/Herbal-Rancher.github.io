## Key Observations

A network diagram shows the relationship between endpoint devices, intermediary devices, and connection media. Identifying the LAN and WAN boundaries helps explain how local traffic reaches remote networks.

### Activity Focus

1. Inspect the device categories and connection media in the Packet Tracer toolbar.
2. Count and classify endpoint and intermediary devices in the supplied topology.
3. Describe the role of servers and intermediary devices, and criteria for selecting media.
4. Compare the LANs and WANs shown with real network and internet connections.
5. Optionally add devices or build a small network to explore further.

### Notes

Counts depend on the supplied topology; the written document alone does not show its icons. Record what you observe in your activity.
