# Targeted Review

Each exam domain now has a separate, detailed study page. The individual pages provide definitions, examples, commands, troubleshooting clues, memory rules, and knowledge checks without creating one extremely long mobile page.

> **Study loop:** Study one domain → explain it aloud in your own words → run that domain in **Focused Review** → study missed concepts → use **Retest Missed**.

<div class="domain-card">
<strong>Domain 1 — Routing & EIGRP</strong><br>
Routing decisions, static and dynamic routes, EIGRP neighbor formation, convergence, DUAL, successors, floating static routes, OSPF, RIP, BGP, and verification.<br>
<a href="/network-portfolio/sf-1041-network-exam-prep/review/routing-eigrp/">Study Domain 1</a>
</div>

<div class="domain-card">
<strong>Domain 2 — Switching, VLANs & Redundancy</strong><br>
MAC forwarding, VLANs, trunks, inter-VLAN routing, STP/RSTP, EtherChannel, LACP, redundancy, and switch troubleshooting.<br>
<a href="/network-portfolio/sf-1041-network-exam-prep/review/switching-vlans/">Study Domain 2</a>
</div>

<div class="domain-card">
<strong>Domain 3 — Secure Wireless & 802.1X</strong><br>
Wireless design, RF behavior, WPA2/WPA3, 802.1X roles, EAP, RADIUS, authentication failures, roaming, and troubleshooting.<br>
<a href="/network-portfolio/sf-1041-network-exam-prep/review/wireless-802-1x/">Study Domain 3</a>
</div>

<div class="domain-card">
<strong>Domain 4 — Firewalls, ACLs & Secure Management</strong><br>
Firewall decisions, ACL order, stateful inspection, NAT/PAT, segmentation, IDS/IPS, secure device management, and validation.<br>
<a href="/network-portfolio/sf-1041-network-exam-prep/review/firewalls-security/">Study Domain 4</a>
</div>

<div class="domain-card">
<strong>Domain 5 — Services, Tools & Monitoring</strong><br>
DHCP, DNS, NTP, SNMP, Syslog, SIEM, important ports, command-line tools, baselines, and monitoring evidence.<br>
<a href="/network-portfolio/sf-1041-network-exam-prep/review/services-monitoring/">Study Domain 5</a>
</div>

<div class="domain-card">
<strong>Domain 6 — Troubleshooting & Analytical Thinking</strong><br>
Structured troubleshooting, scope and layer isolation, evidence-based testing, performance symptoms, change control, verification, and escalation.<br>
<a href="/network-portfolio/sf-1041-network-exam-prep/review/troubleshooting/">Study Domain 6</a>
</div>

<div class="domain-card">
<strong>Domain 7 — Multifunction Devices & Documentation</strong><br>
Networked printers and scanners, print protocols, scan-to-email/folder dependencies, diagrams, baselines, runbooks, change records, and root-cause analysis.<br>
<a href="/network-portfolio/sf-1041-network-exam-prep/review/mfp-documentation/">Study Domain 7</a>
</div>
