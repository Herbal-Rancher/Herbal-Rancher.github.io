## Overview

A structured approach to diagnosing intermittent connectivity, slow network performance, and physical interface problems in an office network.

---

## Assessment Focus

* Identifying the scope of physical connectivity problems
* Inspecting Ethernet cabling, connectors, and patch panels
* Checking switch link lights and interface errors
* Testing cabling and network paths
* Investigating interference and port configuration
* Verifying repairs and documenting findings

## Reflection

This discussion reinforced the importance of checking the physical network before making higher-layer configuration changes. I practiced connecting symptoms, interface statistics, and cable-testing evidence to a systematic troubleshooting process.
