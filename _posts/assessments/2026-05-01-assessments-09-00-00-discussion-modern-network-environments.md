## Overview

An examination of IoT, cloud computing, SD-WAN, 5G, and AI and how these technologies change enterprise networking and security requirements.

---

## Assessment Focus

* IoT devices and endpoint growth
* Cloud and hybrid network connectivity
* SD-WAN and distributed networks
* 5G and mobile connectivity
* AI-assisted monitoring and automation
* Segmentation, firmware management, and secure access

## Reflection

This discussion helped me consider how modern technologies expand both network capabilities and security responsibilities. IoT was especially useful for connecting practical infrastructure requirements—such as PoE, addressing, and bandwidth—with access control and device security.
