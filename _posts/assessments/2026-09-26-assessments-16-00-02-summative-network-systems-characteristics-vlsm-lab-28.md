## Overview

A Packet Tracer VLSM addressing exercise using the 10.1.1.0/24 network to design efficient subnets and configure routers, switches, and hosts.

---

## Assessment Focus

* Analyze host-address requirements
* Calculate VLSM subnets and prefix lengths
* Allocate contiguous subnet ranges
* Configure router and switch IP addressing
* Configure host gateways
* Verify end-to-end IP connectivity

## Reflection

Lab 28 helped me move from calculating VLSM subnets to implementing them on actual simulated devices. The most important lesson was to size subnets by usable host requirements and then verify that the addressing plan works across the complete network.
