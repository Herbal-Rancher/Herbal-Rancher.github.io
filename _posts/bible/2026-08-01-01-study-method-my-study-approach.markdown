Alongside my technical studies, I am committed to a structured and
intentional approach to studying Scripture. I am beginning in Genesis
and working through the Bible systematically using an inductive,
Precept-style approach.

This method emphasizes careful **observation**, thoughtful
**interpretation**, and practical **application**. I want to engage
directly with the biblical text, compare Scripture with Scripture,
distinguish explicit statements from inference, and document how my
understanding develops.

<!--more-->

---

## What Is Inductive Study?

The inductive method is built on three key steps:

-   **Observation** -- Identifying what the text says by noting key
    words, themes, and structure
-   **Interpretation** -- Understanding what the text means in its
    proper context
-   **Application** -- Applying the insights to personal growth and
    daily life

This process encourages active learning, critical thinking, and
consistency ... skills that also directly support my development in
technical fields like networking.

---

## How This Fits Into My Learning Journey

Just as I document my progress in networking through structured lab work
and portfolio development, I approach my Bible study with the same level
of discipline and reflection.

Both areas of study reinforce:

-   Analytical thinking
-   Attention to detail
-   Structured problem-solving
-   Consistency in learning

This dual approach reflects my commitment to both **technical and
personal growth**.
