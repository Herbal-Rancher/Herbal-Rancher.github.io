## Observation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## In the Beginning --- Slowing Down to See

I'm currently in the **observation phase** of my inductive Bible study
using a Precept-style approach. I've spent several weeks in Genesis 1,
and instead of rushing forward, I'm choosing to slow down even more. \*
Read [Genesis Intro](https://www.bible.com/bible/111/GEN.INTRO1.NIV) \*
Read and Listen to [Genesis 1](https://www.bible.com/bible/111/GEN.1.NIV) (2 full times)


<!--more-->

Before moving into deeper interpretation and application, I'm planning
to **listen to Genesis twice in full**, then return again to **Genesis
1:2**. My goal is to step back, gain a broader view, and then come back
with sharper awareness.

Even though it feels slow, I'm beginning to see patterns I would have
missed otherwise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What I'm Beginning to See

In just observation alone, I'm noticing how God reveals Himself through
repeated actions and roles:

-   **God the Father** --- Creator, Maker
-   **The Holy Spirit** --- Moving, hovering, present over the deep
-   **The Word** --- God *speaking*, calling, bringing things into
    existence

There is a rhythm forming:

-   God **speaks**
-   Creation **responds**
-   God **sees**
-   God **calls/names**
-   God **blesses**

This pattern is steady, intentional, and powerful.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Personal Reflection (Observation Only)

One thing that is standing out to me:

> When I feel like I'm in darkness, in deep or formless places, the Holy
> Spirit is not absent --- He is already there, **hovering, waiting for
> God to speak.**

This connects deeply with:

> *"In the beginning was the Word..."* (John 1:1)

God speaks --- and things change.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Current Focus

Right now, I'm not trying to interpret everything.

I'm focusing on:

-   Repetition
-   Structure
-   Key actions (said, saw, called, blessed)
-   Order of creation
-   Relationships between what is spoken and what happens

## Closing Thought

Even in the very beginning, before light, before structure ---

**God was already present. The Spirit was already moving. And the Word
was already active.**

I'm learning that slowing down isn't falling behind ... it's how I can
begin to truly see.
