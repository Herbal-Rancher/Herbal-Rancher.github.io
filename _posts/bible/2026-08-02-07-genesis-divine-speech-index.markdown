> **Research Question:** When Genesis describes God speaking or
> communicating, what patterns emerge---and how do those patterns relate
> to the New Testament revelation of Jesus Christ as the Word of God?

I trace the Hebrew wording, speaker, recipient, method, function,
result, and fulfillment. I observe Genesis first and compare with later
Scripture second.

Evidence:
* 🟢 Explicit
* 🔵 Strong Connection
* 🟡 Inference
* ⚪ Open Question
* 🔴 Unsupported / Speculative

## Investigation Guardrail

I am not assuming that every divine quotation or appearance is
specifically the pre-incarnate Jesus. I am tracing what Genesis says
first, then asking where later Scripture provides legitimate grounds for
a Christological connection.
