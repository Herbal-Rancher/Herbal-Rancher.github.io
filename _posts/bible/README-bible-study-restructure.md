## Important URL Migration

The updated pages move Bible content out of
`/network-portfolio/bible/...` and into `/bible-study/...`.

If existing public URLs are already indexed or shared, preserve
redirects from the old permalinks rather than simply deleting them.

## Numbering Pattern

For chapter studies, the updated metadata supports:

`TESTAMENT.BOOK.CHAPTER.STUDY`

Example:

-   `01.01.01.01` = Old Testament → Genesis → Chapter 1 → Observation
-   `01.01.01.02` = Old Testament → Genesis → Chapter 1 → Divine Speech

The visible page title remains reader-friendly; the numbering is
primarily organizational metadata.
