## Overview

This study diagram compares the major dynamic routing protocols **RIP, OSPF, EIGRP, IS-IS, and BGP**, including how each protocol learns routes and determines the best path through a network.

<!--more-->

The diagram helped me understand the differences between distance-vector, link-state, advanced distance-vector, and path-vector routing and how routing metrics, administrative distance, autonomous systems, and routing policies affect route selection.
