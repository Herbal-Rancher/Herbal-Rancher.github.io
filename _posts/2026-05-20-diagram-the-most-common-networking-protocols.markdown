## Overview

This study diagram provides a quick-reference overview of common networking protocols and the roles they perform when devices communicate across networks.

It begins with **FTP, HTTP, HTTPS, SMTP, DHCP, and SSH**, then connects those application and network services to the underlying **TCP, UDP, and IP** protocols.

<!--more-->

The diagram also highlights common port numbers, secure versus insecure communication, and how the protocols work together from the application layer through the transport and network layers.
