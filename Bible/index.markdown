Welcome to my Bible Study journey. This is my lifelong-learning record of reading Scripture carefully, documenting what I observe, comparing Scripture with Scripture, asking and testing questions, and allowing the biblical text to refine my conclusions.

Like my Network+ journey, these pages document **what I am learning and how I am learning it**. My goal is not to present myself as having every answer, but to leave a clear evidence trail that I—and other readers—can follow.

I partner with **ChatGPT as an AI-assisted study and research partner** to explore questions, discover connections, examine context, challenge assumptions, and organize my developing studies. AI helps me investigate and think more deeply, but it is **a study partner—not an authority**.

**Scripture remains my primary source and final reference point.** I return to the biblical text to test ideas, verify connections, seek truth and clarity, and revise my understanding when the evidence requires it.

My approach is simple: **Observe carefully. Ask questions. Investigate. Compare Scripture with Scripture. Test interpretations. Follow the evidence. Seek truth. Remain willing to learn.**

## Inductive Bible Study


My study process follows three foundational steps:

- **Observation** — What does the text say?
- **Interpretation** — What does the text mean in its context?
- **Application** — How should what I have learned affect understanding, faith, and life?

As deeper questions emerge, I may also trace original-language words, cross-references, repeated themes, covenant patterns, historical context, and connections later biblical writers make to earlier Scripture.

Throughout these studies, I aim to distinguish:

* **🟢 Explicit**
* **🔵 Strong Biblical Connection**
* **🟡 Inference**
* **⚪ Open Question**
* **🔴 Unsupported / Speculative**

## How the Journey Is Organized

**Bible Study → Testament → Book Group → Book → Study Series → Chapter
Study → Book Conclusion**

Each biblical book becomes its own learning journey. Chapter pages hold the
detailed work; the book page keeps the larger story visible and becomes
the final synthesis when the book is complete.

## The Purpose of This Journey

This is an ongoing study—not a finished statement of everything I believe.

I want to read slowly enough to notice what is actually there, remain willing to revise my understanding when Scripture requires it, and document the journey honestly as I move from book to book.

> **Observe first. Understand in context. Compare Scripture with Scripture. Test conclusions. Let the evidence lead.**
